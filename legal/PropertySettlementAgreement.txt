PROPERTY SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT

This Property Settlement Agreement ("Agreement") is made and entered into this ___ day of ____________, 2024, by and between:

John Lawrence Scerba, residing at 3635 Gill St, Chester, Virginia, 23831 ("Party A"), and  
Amy Jessica Brick, residing at 3635 Gill St, Chester, Virginia, 23831 ("Party B").

Collectively referred to as the "Parties."

---

RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Parties are co-owners of the real property located at 3635 Gill St, Chester, Virginia, 23831 (the "Property"), Tax ID: 792655276300000, legally described as follows:

[Insert full legal property description from deed];

WHEREAS, the Parties acknowledge that Party B owns 19% and Party A owns 81% of the Property as tenants in common;

WHEREAS, the Parties desire to formally terminate their co-ownership of the Property, with Party B purchasing Party A’s interest;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises, covenants, and good and valuable consideration contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

---

1. Ownership and Title  
The Property is currently held as tenants in common. The Parties own undivided interests in the Property as follows:  
- Party B: 19%  
- Party A: 81%

---

2. Buyout Terms  
Party B shall purchase Party A's interest for the sum of $251,910, payable by refinance loan proceeds on or before July 31, 2025.  
If payment is not made by the stated date, this Agreement shall automatically terminate and neither Party shall have any further obligation hereunder, except for obligations expressly stated to survive termination.

The Parties agree to coordinate the payment and the execution and delivery of the necessary conveyance documents through a mutually agreed escrow agent to ensure the simultaneous exchange of funds and deed.

---

3. New Financing  
Party B may obtain new financing to complete this buyout through a refinance loan.

---

4. Transfer of Interest  
Party A agrees to execute any deed or other instruments reasonably necessary to convey all of his interest in the Property to Party B within three (3) business days of receiving full payment under this Agreement through escrow. Title and possession shall transfer upon recordation of such instruments.

---

5. Possession  
Upon completion of the transfer, Party B shall have exclusive possession of the Property.

---

6. Prorations  
All real estate taxes, homeowners' association dues (if any), and other assessments shall be prorated as of the date of transfer.

---

7. Release of Claims  
Upon completion of the buyout and transfer of interest, the Parties mutually release and discharge each other from any and all present and future claims, obligations, or liabilities relating to the Property.

---

8. Disclosure of Liens  
The Parties affirm that the Property is currently not subject to any liens or encumbrances other than non-delinquent real estate taxes, non-delinquent assessments, and homeowners' association dues (if applicable). If any undisclosed liens are discovered, the responsible Party shall promptly satisfy, indemnify, and hold harmless the other Party from any liability arising therefrom.

---

9. Expenses  
Party B shall bear all customary costs associated with the transfer of the Property, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

---

10. No Representations or Warranties  
Each Party acknowledges and agrees that, in entering into this Agreement, they are not relying on any representation, statement, or warranty not expressly set forth herein.

---

11. Governing Law  
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Commonwealth of Virginia.

---

12. Binding Effect  
This Agreement shall be binding upon and inure to the benefit of the Parties and their respective heirs, executors, administrators, successors, and assigns.

---

13. Survival of Certain Obligations  
The representations and obligations contained in Section 8 (Disclosure of Liens) shall survive the execution of this Agreement and the transfer of interest.

---

14. Further Assurances  
Each Party agrees to execute and deliver such further documents and instruments as may reasonably be required to fully effectuate the terms and intent of this Agreement.

---

15. Severability  
If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

---

16. Entire Agreement  
This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties concerning the Property and supersedes all prior agreements, oral or written, relating thereto.

---

17. Attorney Review and Waiver of Counsel  
Each Party acknowledges that they have been advised of their right to consult with independent legal counsel regarding this Agreement and either has consulted with counsel or knowingly and voluntarily waived such right.

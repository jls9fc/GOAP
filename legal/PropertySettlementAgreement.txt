RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Parties are co-owners of the real property located at [Full Property Address], Virginia, legally described as follows (the "Property"):

[Insert full legal property description from deed];

WHEREAS, the Parties desire to formally terminate their co-ownership of the Property, with [Party A/Party B] purchasing the other’s interest;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises and covenants contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

---

1. Ownership and Title  
The Property is currently held as tenants in common. The Parties own undivided interests in the Property as follows:  
- [Party A]: [Insert percentage]%  
- [Party B]: [Insert percentage]%

---

2. Buyout Terms  
[Party A/Party B] agrees to buy out [Party B/Party A] for the total sum of $__________. Payment shall be made via [e.g., lump sum / refinance proceeds] on or before ____________, 20__.  
If payment is not made by the stated date, this Agreement shall be void unless otherwise agreed in writing by the Parties.

---

3. New Financing (If Applicable)  
[Party A/Party B] may obtain new financing to complete this buyout, but there is no existing mortgage on the Property to refinance.

---

4. Transfer of Interest  
[Party B/Party A] agrees to execute a Quitclaim Deed conveying all of their interest in the Property to [Party A/Party B] within three (3) business days of receiving full payment under this Agreement. Title and possession shall transfer upon recordation of the Quitclaim Deed.

---

5. Possession  
Upon completion of the transfer, [Party A/Party B] shall have exclusive possession of the Property.

---

6. Prorations  
All real estate taxes, homeowners' association dues (if any), and other assessments shall be prorated as of the date of transfer.

---

7. Release of Claims  
Upon completion of the buyout and transfer of interest, the Parties mutually release and discharge each other from any and all present and future claims, obligations, or liabilities relating to the Property.

---

8. Disclosure of Liens  
The Parties affirm that the Property is currently not subject to any liens or encumbrances other than standard property taxes, assessments, and homeowners' association dues (if applicable). If any undisclosed liens are discovered, the responsible Party shall promptly satisfy or resolve them.

---

9. Expenses  
The Parties agree that [Party A/Party B] shall bear all costs associated with this transaction, including but not limited to any legal fees, deed preparation, recording fees, and any financing costs, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

---

10. No Representations or Warranties  
Each Party acknowledges and agrees that, in entering into this Agreement, they are not relying on any representation, statement, or warranty not expressly set forth herein.

---

11. Governing Law  
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Commonwealth of Virginia.

---

12. Binding Effect  
This Agreement shall be binding upon and inure to the benefit of the Parties and their respective heirs, executors, administrators, successors, and assigns.

---

13. Survival of Certain Obligations  
The representations and obligations contained in Section 8 (Disclosure of Liens) shall survive the execution of this Agreement and the transfer of interest.

---

14. Entire Agreement  
This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties concerning the Property and supersedes all prior agreements, oral or written, relating thereto.

---

15. Attorney Review and Waiver of Counsel  
Each Party acknowledges that they have been advised of their right to consult with independent legal counsel regarding this Agreement and either has consulted with counsel or knowingly and voluntarily waived such right.
